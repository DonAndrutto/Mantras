# The original designs

The two Claude Design projects the visualisations were taken from, kept for reference.
They are not loaded by the page.

- `vajrasattva/` — *Mantra Garland*: the hundred-syllable mantra around ཧཱུྃ, the short
  mantra standing still around the seed. `garland.jsx` holds the drawing; the
  `.dc.html` file holds the text and the default angle and speed.
- `vajra-guru/` — *Vajra Guru Garland*: the mantra on the outside wall of a ring
  around ཧྲཱིཿ, with a Tibetan / Latin switch. `garland-guru.jsx` holds the drawing.

The values carried over into `mantras/*.js`: colours, letter and seed sizes,
gaps, opacity by depth, the front highlight, the breathing of the seed's glow,
camera distance and default angle and speed.
